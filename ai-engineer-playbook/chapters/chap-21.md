## 2. Problem

You are an AIE at acme-corp. The engineering manager has just told you: "influencing at the ai function. 30-day timeline."

## 3. Why AIEs Fail Here

Five named failure modes of AIEs whose influencing at the ai function produced zero results.

- **The No-Stakeholder-Map Failure.** No stakeholder map.
- **The Slack-Channel Failure.** Drops AI features in Slack.
- **The No-Framework Failure.** Opinions, not frameworks.
- **The No-Data Failure.** Opinions, not data.
- **The No-Relationship Failure.** Only talks in crisis.

## 8. Failure Mode Postmortem

An AIE at a 200-person B2B AI company had no stakeholder map. Dropped features in Slack. The PM was out of the loop. 0 features shipped.

What the first AIE missed: influencing at the ai function is a system. The first AIE had no system. The second AIE had 4 mental models + 3 frameworks + 5 criteria. The system is the leverage.

The lesson: the AIE who has the system has influencing at the ai function. The AIE who has no system has the failure mode.
