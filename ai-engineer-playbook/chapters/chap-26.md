## 2. Problem

You are an AIE at acme-corp. The engineering manager has just told you: "the 30/60/90 for the new aie. 30-day timeline."

This chapter tells you the 4 mental models, 3 frameworks, and 5-criterion quality bar.

**Decision in one sentence:** _AIE 30/60/90 is a 30-day listen + learn + 60-day design + align + 90-day execute + review; the AIE's job is to listen first, design second, execute third._

---

## 3. Why AIEs Fail Here

Five named failure modes of AIEs whose the 30/60/90 for the new aie produced zero results.

- **The No-Listen Failure.** Starts changing things immediately.
- **The No-Learn Failure.** Doesn't learn the LLM stack.
- **The No-Alignment Failure.** No stakeholder alignment.
- **The Over-Promise Failure.** Over-promises in first 30 days.
- **The No-Q1-OKRs Failure.** No Q1 OKRs.

---

## 4. Mental Models

Four mental models that compress the 30/60/90 for the new aie.

**mental model 1: The 30-Day Listen + Learn:** 30 days: meet EM + read LLM docs + top 3 issues.

**mental model 2: The 60-Day Design + Align:** 60 days: design LLM systems + align stakeholders + cascade OKRs.

**mental model 3: The 90-Day Execute + Review:** 90 days: ship features + run Q1 OKRs + 90-day review.

**mental model 4: The Top 3 Issues Template:** Per issue detail.

## 8. Failure Mode Postmortem

A new AIE at a 200-person B2B AI company started changing things immediately. No listening. The team disengaged. Q1 OKRs missed by 50%.

What the first AIE missed: the 30/60/90 for the new aie is a system. The first AIE had no system. The second AIE had 4 mental models + 3 frameworks + 5 criteria. The system is the leverage.

The lesson: the AIE who has the system has the 30/60/90 for the new aie. The AIE who has no system has the failure mode.
