## 3. Why AIEs Fail Here

Five named failure modes of AIEs whose ai feature revenue impact produced zero results.

- **The No-Revenue-Alignment Failure.** Features don't know revenue.
- **The 1-GTM-Motion Failure.** Features support only 1 motion.
- **The No-Customer-Outcome Failure.** No customer outcome tracking.
- **The No-Adoption-Tracking Failure.** No adoption tracking.
- **The No-Expansion Failure.** No expansion tracking.

---

## 4. Mental Models

Four mental models that compress ai feature revenue impact.

**mental model 1: The 4 Revenue Pillars:** 4 pillars: features + adoption + retention + expansion.

**mental model 2: The 3 GTM Motions:** 3 motions: land + expand + retain.

**mental model 3: The 5-Criterion Bar:** 5 criteria: aligned + specific + measured + owned + timed.

**mental model 4: The Revenue Traceability Chain:** Feature -> adoption -> revenue.

## 8. Failure Mode Postmortem

An AIE at a 200-person B2B AI company had no revenue alignment. 5 AI features shipped but only 1 was tied to revenue ($100K ARR vs $500K target).

What the first AIE missed: ai feature revenue impact is a system. The first AIE had no system. The second AIE had 4 mental models + 3 frameworks + 5 criteria. The system is the leverage.

The lesson: the AIE who has the system has ai feature revenue impact. The AIE who has no system has the failure mode.
