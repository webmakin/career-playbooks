## 1. Epigraph

---

## 2. Problem

You are an AIE at acme-corp. The engineering manager has just told you: "aie-pm partnership. 30-day timeline."

This chapter tells you the 4 mental models, 3 frameworks, and 5-criterion quality bar.

**Decision in one sentence:** _AIE-PM partnership is a 4-ownership-boundary model + 3 cadence patterns + 5-criterion bar; the AIE's job is to provide high-quality LLM features, accept PM's product decisions, and own the LLM-to-product handoff._

---

## 3. Why AIEs Fail Here

Five named failure modes of AIEs whose aie-pm partnership produced zero results.

- **The Throwing-Over-Wall Failure.** AIE hands feature to PM with no support.
- **The No-Handoff Failure.** No handoff process.
- **The AIE-Tells-PM-What-To-Build Failure.** AIE dictates features.
- **The PM-Tells-AIE-What-To-LLM Failure.** PM dictates LLM.
- **The No-Cadence Failure.** No sync.

---

## 4. Mental Models

Four mental models that compress aie-pm partnership.

**mental model 1: The 4 Ownership Boundaries:** 4 boundaries: AIE (LLM stack + prompts + eval) + PM (feature roadmap + customer narrative + launch).

**mental model 2: The 3 Cadence Patterns:** 3 cadences: weekly 30 min + monthly 60 min + quarterly 90 min.

**mental model 3: The 5-Criterion Bar:** 5 criteria: aligned + specific + measured + owned + reusable.

**mental model 4: The Feature Brief:** 1-page feature brief.

## 8. Failure Mode Postmortem

An AIE at a 200-person B2B AI company threw LLM features over the wall to PM. PM struggled. Customer confused. The CTO said: 'No handoff, no LLM features.'

What the first AIE missed: aie-pm partnership is a system. The first AIE had no system. The second AIE had 4 mental models + 3 frameworks + 5 criteria. The system is the leverage.

The lesson: the AIE who has the system has aie-pm partnership. The AIE who has no system has the failure mode.
