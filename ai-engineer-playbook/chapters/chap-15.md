## 3. Why AIEs Fail Here

Five named failure modes of AIEs whose aie strategy at company scale produced zero results.

- **The No-Strategy-Input Failure.** AIE has no strategy input.
- **The 1-Horizon Failure.** NOW only.
- **The No-Cost-Analysis Failure.** No cost analysis.
- **The No-Stakeholder-Influence Failure.** No influence map.
- **The Reactive-Work Failure.** Reactive, not proactive.

## 8. Failure Mode Postmortem

An AIE at a 200-person B2B AI company had no strategy input. The CTO's AI direction was unclear. 0 features.

What the first AIE missed: aie strategy at company scale is a system. The first AIE had no system. The second AIE had 4 mental models + 3 frameworks + 5 criteria. The system is the leverage.

The lesson: the AIE who has the system has aie strategy at company scale. The AIE who has no system has the failure mode.
