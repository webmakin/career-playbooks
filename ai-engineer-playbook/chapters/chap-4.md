## 1. Epigraph

## 2. Problem

You are an AIE at acme-corp. The engineering manager has just told you: "aie stakeholder management. 30-day timeline."

This chapter tells you the 4 mental models, 3 frameworks, and 5-criterion quality bar.

**Decision in one sentence:** _AIE stakeholder management is a 4-group stakeholder map + 3 cadence patterns + 5-criterion bar; the AIE's job is to identify stakeholders, run the cadences, and own the relationships._

---

## 3. Why AIEs Fail Here

Five named failure modes of AIEs whose aie stakeholder management produced zero results.

- **The No-Stakeholder-Map Failure.** No stakeholder map.
- **The Slack-Channel Failure.** Drops features in Slack.
- **The No-Customer-Outcome Failure.** Doesn't tie features to customers.
- **The No-PM-Relationship Failure.** Doesn't engage with PM.
- **The No-MLR-Relationship Failure.** Doesn't engage with MLR.

---

## 4. Mental Models

Four mental models that compress aie stakeholder management.

**mental model 1: The 4 Stakeholder Groups:** 4 groups: PM + EM + senior MLR + customers.

**mental model 2: The 3 Cadence Patterns:** 3 cadences: weekly 1:1 with PM + monthly with EM + quarterly with MLR.

**mental model 3: The 5-Criterion Bar:** 5 criteria: aligned + specific + measured + owned + reusable.

**mental model 4: The Stakeholder Map:** Per-stakeholder detail.

## 8. Failure Mode Postmortem

An AIE at a 200-person B2B AI company had no stakeholder map. Dropped features in Slack. PM was unaware. Customers churning.

What the first AIE missed: aie stakeholder management is a system. The first AIE had no system. The second AIE had 4 mental models + 3 frameworks + 5 criteria. The system is the leverage.

The lesson: the AIE who has the system has aie stakeholder management. The AIE who has no system has the failure mode.
