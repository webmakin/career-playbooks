# ML Researcher Playbook

## Preface: How to Read This Book

> **The ML Researcher role is the operating layer between research curiosity and engineering execution at the AI frontier.** This book is for the ML engineer who wants to make the leap to MLR, the new MLR in their first 90 days, or the MLR contributing to research direction at scale.

### How the book is organized

The book has **4 parts and 28 chapters**, plus this preface, glossary, and errata:

- **Part I — Foundations (Ch 1-9).** What an MLR is, the role, the IC-to-MLR category change, stakeholder management, experimental design, paper writing, benchmarking, OSS, productivity.
- **Part II — Execution (Ch 10-17).** Hypothesis, architecture, training, inference, MLR-eng collaboration, strategy, build vs buy, research-revenue.
- **Part III — Scale (Ch 18-21).** Org contribution, hiring, performance, influence.
- **Part IV — Governance & Portfolio (Ch 22-28).** Risk, compliance, crisis, auditability, the 30/60/90, the portfolio map, methodology appendix.

### The 11-section chapter anatomy

Every chapter follows the same 11-section structure:

1. **Epigraph** — the one-sentence thesis.
2. **Problem** — the situation + decision in one sentence.
3. **Why MLRs Fail Here** — 5 named failure modes.
4. **Mental Models** — 4 mental models with diagrams.
5. **Frameworks** — 3 reusable frameworks.
6. **Drill** — a 90-minute hands-on exercise.
7. **Worked Example** — a real-world application.
8. **Failure Mode Postmortem** — what the failure looks like.
9. **Self-Assessment Rubric** — 5-dimension, 25-point rubric.
10. **Portfolio Artifact Note** — what to save as interview evidence.
11. **Interview Questions** — 5 questions you should be able to answer.

### How to read it

1. **If you're aspiring to be an MLR:** Read Part I + Part IV Chapter 27 (Portfolio Map).
2. **If you're a new MLR in your first 90 days:** Read Part I + Part IV Chapter 26 (30/60/90).
3. **If you're an MLR scaling 5 → 10 papers/year:** Read Part II (Execution).

### A note on opinion

This book is opinionated. The 4-pillar MLR model (research methodology + experimental design + paper pipeline + research direction) is one MLR's framework. Adapt what doesn't fit your context.

### Let's go.

The MLR role is one of the most leveraged roles in any AI-forward company. An MLR who runs 5 experiments per week at 100% reproducibility with 2 papers submitted per year and 3+ co-authors per paper is a force multiplier. The 28 chapters in this book are the leverage. The system is the discipline. The outcome is the research output.
