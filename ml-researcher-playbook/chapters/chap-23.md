## 3. Why MLRs Fail Here

Five named failure modes of MLRs whose mlr compliance and ethics produced zero results.

- **The No-Compliance-Awareness Failure.** No compliance awareness.
- **The Annual-Only Failure.** Annual review only.
- **The No-IRB Failure.** No IRB review.
- **The No-AI-Safety Failure.** No AI safety review.
- **The No-Owner Failure.** No compliance owner per MLR.

## 8. Failure Mode Postmortem

An MLR at a 200-person B2B AI company had no compliance awareness. IRB review missed. The senior scientist said: 'IRB is non-negotiable.'

What the first MLR missed: mlr compliance and ethics is a system. The first MLR had no system. The second MLR had 4 mental models + 3 frameworks + 5 criteria. The system is the leverage.

The lesson: the MLR who has the system has mlr compliance and ethics. The MLR who has no system has the failure mode.
