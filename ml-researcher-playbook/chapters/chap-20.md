## 2. Problem

You are an MLR at acme-corp. The senior scientist has just told you: "mlr performance management. 30-day timeline."

This chapter tells you the 4 mental models, 3 frameworks, and 5-criterion quality bar.

**Decision in one sentence:** _MLR performance participation is a 5-dim rubric + 3-tier calibration + quarterly cadence + 12-month dev plan; the MLR's job is to track own performance, contribute to calibration, and own the dev plan._

---

## 3. Why MLRs Fail Here

Five named failure modes of MLRs whose mlr performance management produced zero results.

- **The SWE-Rubric Failure.** 1-2 dims only.
- **The No-Self-Tracking Failure.** No self-tracking.
- **The No-Cadence Failure.** Annual only.
- **The No-Dev-Plan Failure.** No 12-month plan.
- **The No-Promo-Pipeline Failure.** No promotion criteria.

---

## 4. Mental Models

Four mental models that compress mlr performance management.

**mental model 1: The 5-Dim Research Rubric:** 5 dims: publication + research depth + engineering + collaboration + impact.

**mental model 2: The 3-Tier Calibration:** 3 tiers.

**mental model 3: The Quarterly Cadence:** 4 quarters.

**mental model 4: The 12-Month Dev Plan:** 1 plan per MLR.

## 8. Failure Mode Postmortem

An MLR at a 200-person B2B AI company had no self-tracking. The annual review was a surprise. The MLR was below bar. The senior scientist said: 'Self-track quarterly.'

What the first MLR missed: mlr performance management is a system. The first MLR had no system. The second MLR had 4 mental models + 3 frameworks + 5 criteria. The system is the leverage.

The lesson: the MLR who has the system has mlr performance management. The MLR who has no system has the failure mode.

## 9. Self-Assessment Rubric

| 1 | **5-dim rubric** | 1-2 | 3-4 | 5 dims |
| 2 | **3-tier calibration** | 1 | 2 | 3 tiers |
| 3 | **Quarterly cadence** | Annual | Semi-annual | Quarterly |
| 4 | **12-month dev plan** | None | Plan exists | 1 plan per MLR |
| 5 | **Promotion tracking** | None | Pipeline exists | 1+ promotion target |


**Disqualifier:** any 1 on dimension 1 or 3. An MLR who has 1-2 dims or annual only is in the SWE-Rubric or No-Cadence failure mode.
