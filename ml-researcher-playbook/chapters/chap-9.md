## 2. Problem

You are an MLR at acme-corp. The senior scientist has just told you: "mlr productivity and time management. 30-day timeline."

This chapter tells you the 4 mental models, 3 frameworks, and 5-criterion quality bar.

**Decision in one sentence:** _MLR productivity is a 4-pillar time framework + 3-cycle cadence + 5-criterion bar; the MLR's job is to allocate time, run the cadence, and own the research output._

---

## 3. Why MLRs Fail Here

Five named failure modes of MLRs whose mlr productivity and time management produced zero results.

- **The All-Engineering Failure.** 100% engineering. 0 papers.
- **The All-Writing Failure.** 100% writing. 0 experiments.
- **The No-Cadence Failure.** Ad-hoc work.
- **The Context-Switching Failure.** 5+ experiments in parallel. None ship.
- **The No-Direction Failure.** Reactive work.

---

## 4. Mental Models

Four mental models that compress mlr productivity and time management.

**mental model 1: The 4-Pillar Time Framework:** 4 pillars: research + engineering + writing + collaboration.

**mental model 2: The 3-Cycle Cadence:** 3 cycles: weekly + monthly + quarterly.

**mental model 3: The 5-Criterion Productivity Bar:** 5 criteria: experiments + papers + impact + collaboration + direction.

**mental model 4: The Weekly Plan Template:** 1-page weekly plan.

## 8. Failure Mode Postmortem

An MLR at a 200-person B2B AI company spent 80% on engineering. 0 papers. The senior scientist said: 'Your time is unbalanced.'

What the first MLR missed: mlr productivity and time management is a system. The first MLR had no system. The second MLR had 4 mental models + 3 frameworks + 5 criteria. The system is the leverage.

The lesson: the MLR who has the system has mlr productivity and time management. The MLR who has no system has the failure mode.
