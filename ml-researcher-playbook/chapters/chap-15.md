## 2. Problem

You are an MLR at acme-corp. The senior scientist has just told you: "mlr strategy at company scale. 30-day timeline."

**Decision in one sentence:** _MLR research strategy contribution is a 4-input framework + 3 horizon levels + 5-criterion bar; the MLR's job is to provide high-quality research input, accept the senior scientist's strategy, and own specific strategy items._

## 3. Why MLRs Fail Here

Five named failure modes of MLRs whose mlr strategy at company scale produced zero results.

- **The No-Strategy-Input Failure.** MLR has no strategy input.
- **The 1-Horizon Failure.** NOW only.
- **The No-Capacity Failure.** No capacity numbers.
- **The No-Stakeholder-Influence Failure.** No influence map.
- **The Reactive-Work Failure.** Reactive, not proactive.

---

## 4. Mental Models

Four mental models that compress mlr strategy at company scale.

**mental model 1: The 4 Strategy Inputs:** 4 inputs: research + paper + architecture + collaboration.

**mental model 2: The 3 Horizon Levels:** 3 levels: NOW + NEXT + BEYOND.

**mental model 3: The 5-Criterion Bar:** 5 criteria: specific + measured + owned + timed + novel.

**mental model 4: The Strategy Memo:** 1-page memo template.

## 8. Failure Mode Postmortem

An MLR at a 200-person B2B AI company had no strategy input. The senior scientist's direction was unclear. 0 papers.

What the first MLR missed: mlr strategy at company scale is a system. The first MLR had no system. The second MLR had 4 mental models + 3 frameworks + 5 criteria. The system is the leverage.

The lesson: the MLR who has the system has mlr strategy at company scale. The MLR who has no system has the failure mode.
