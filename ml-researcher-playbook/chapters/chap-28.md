## 3. Why MLRs Fail Here

Five named failure modes of MLRs whose research methodology appendix for mlrs produced zero results.

- **The No-Methodology-Review Failure.** No methodology review process.
- **The 1-Method Failure.** 1 method only.
- **The No-Quality-Attribute Failure.** No rigor + reproducibility + novelty review.
- **The Ad-Hoc-Review Failure.** Ad-hoc reviews.
- **The No-Documentation Failure.** No methodology docs.
