## 3. Why MLRs Fail Here

Five named failure modes of MLRs whose mlr hiring and onboarding produced zero results.

- **The No-Interview-Participation Failure.** MLR doesn't interview.
- **The No-Mentorship Failure.** No mentorship of new hires.
- **The No-Onboarding Failure.** No structured onboarding.
- **The 30-Day-Ramp Failure.** No ramp-up.
- **The No-Hire-Bar-Input Failure.** No input on hire bar.

---

## 4. Mental Models

Four mental models that compress mlr hiring and onboarding.

**mental model 1: The 4 Hire Traits:** 4 traits: research depth + publication + engineering + collaboration.

**mental model 2: The 3-Stage Loop:** 3 stages: paper review + technical interview + publication talk.

**mental model 3: The 5-Step Onboarding:** 5 steps: orient + first paper + mentor + lead paper + independent.

**mental model 4: The 30-Day Ramp:** Weekly goals.

## 8. Failure Mode Postmortem

An MLR at a 200-person B2B AI company didn't participate in interviews. New hires were misaligned. 30% attrition in 90 days.

What the first MLR missed: mlr hiring and onboarding is a system. The first MLR had no system. The second MLR had 4 mental models + 3 frameworks + 5 criteria. The system is the leverage.

The lesson: the MLR who has the system has mlr hiring and onboarding. The MLR who has no system has the failure mode.
