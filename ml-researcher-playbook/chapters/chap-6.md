## 3. Why MLRs Fail Here

Five named failure modes of MLRs whose paper writing and submission produced zero results.

- **The No-Story Failure.** No clear paper story. Random experiments.
- **The Weak-Figures Failure.** Low-quality figures.
- **The Weak-Tables Failure.** Missing baseline comparisons.
- **The Missed-Deadlines Failure.** Missed paper deadlines.
- **The No-Rebuttal-Strategy Failure.** No rebuttal strategy.

## 4. Mental Models

Four mental models that compress paper writing and submission.

**mental model 1: The 4 Writing Pillars:** 4 pillars: story + figures + tables + references.

**mental model 2: The 3 Submission Stages:** 3 stages: draft + review + camera-ready.

**mental model 3: The 5-Criterion Paper Bar:** 5 criteria: novel + rigorous + reproducible + impactful + well-written.

**mental model 4: The Paper Template:** 8-section template.

## 8. Failure Mode Postmortem

An MLR at a 200-person B2B AI company wrote papers with no clear story. NeurIPS submissions rejected. The senior scientist said: 'No story, no paper.'

What the first MLR missed: paper writing and submission is a system. The first MLR had no system. The second MLR had 4 mental models + 3 frameworks + 5 criteria. The system is the leverage.

The lesson: the MLR who has the system has paper writing and submission. The MLR who has no system has the failure mode.
