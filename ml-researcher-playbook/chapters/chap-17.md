## 2. Problem

You are an MLR at acme-corp. The senior scientist has just told you: "mlr research-as-revenue. 30-day timeline."

**Decision in one sentence:** _MLR research-revenue alignment is a 4-pillar system + 3 GTM motions + 5-criterion bar; the MLR's job is to align research with revenue, track the pillars, and own the industry voice._

---

## 3. Why MLRs Fail Here

Five named failure modes of MLRs whose mlr research-as-revenue produced zero results.

- **The No-Revenue-Alignment Failure.** Research doesn't know revenue.
- **The 1-GTM-Motion Failure.** Research supports only 1 motion.
- **The No-Customer-Outcome Failure.** No customer outcome tracking.
- **The No-Paper-Revenue Failure.** Papers not linked to revenue.
- **The No-Industry-Voice Failure.** No keynotes or talks.

---

## 4. Mental Models

Four mental models that compress mlr research-as-revenue.

**mental model 1: The 4 Revenue Pillars:** 4 pillars: papers + open-source + benchmarks + industry voice.

**mental model 2: The 3 GTM Motions:** 3 motions: land + expand + retain.

**mental model 3: The 5-Criterion Bar:** 5 criteria: aligned + specific + measured + owned + timed.

**mental model 4: The Revenue Traceability Chain:** Paper -> citation -> adoption -> revenue.

## 8. Failure Mode Postmortem

An MLR at a 200-person B2B AI company had no revenue alignment. 2 papers shipped but neither was tied to revenue. The senior scientist said: 'No revenue, no paper.'

What the first MLR missed: mlr research-as-revenue is a system. The first MLR had no system. The second MLR had 4 mental models + 3 frameworks + 5 criteria. The system is the leverage.

The lesson: the MLR who has the system has mlr research-as-revenue. The MLR who has no system has the failure mode.
