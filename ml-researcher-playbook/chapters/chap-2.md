## 2. Problem

You are an MLR at acme-corp. The senior scientist has just told you: "the ic-to-mlr category change. 30-day timeline."

This chapter tells you the 4 mental models, 3 frameworks, and 5-criterion quality bar.

**Decision in one sentence:** _IC-to-MLR is a 4-mindset-shift category change (ship -> experiment, production -> paper, solo -> collaboration, project -> research direction) with 3-month transition and 5-criterion bar; the MLR's job is to make the 4 shifts and own the research output._

---

## 3. Why MLRs Fail Here

Five named failure modes of MLRs whose the ic-to-mlr category change produced zero results.

- **The Same-as-IC Failure.** MLR works like IC. No category change.
- **The No-Experiments Failure.** 0 experiments. No research rigor.
- **The No-Paper-Focus Failure.** Production focus only.
- **The Solo-Work Failure.** No collaboration. No co-authors.
- **The No-Direction Failure.** No research direction.

---

## 4. Mental Models

Four mental models that compress the ic-to-mlr category change.

**mental model 1: The 4 Mindset Shifts:** 4 shifts: ship->experiment, production->paper, solo->collaboration, project->research.

**mental model 2: The 3-Month Transition:** 3 months: month 1 read papers, month 2 first experiment, month 3 first paper draft.

**mental model 3: The 5-Criterion Transition Bar:** 5 criteria: experiments + papers + collaboration + direction + rigor.

**mental model 4: The MLR vs IC Comparison:** Side-by-side comparison.

## 8. Failure Mode Postmortem

An ML engineer at a 200-person B2B AI company tried to become an MLR. Worked like IC. 0 experiments. 0 papers. The senior scientist said: 'You're still an IC.'

What the first MLR missed: the ic-to-mlr category change is a system. The first MLR had no system. The second MLR had 4 mental models + 3 frameworks + 5 criteria. The system is the leverage.

The lesson: the MLR who has the system has the ic-to-mlr category change. The MLR who has no system has the failure mode.
