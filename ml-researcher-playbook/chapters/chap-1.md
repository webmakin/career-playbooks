## 2. Problem

You are an MLR at acme-corp. The senior scientist has just told you: "what an ml researcher actually does. 30-day timeline."

This chapter tells you the 4 mental models, 3 frameworks, and 5-criterion quality bar.

**Decision in one sentence:** _MLR is a 4-pillar system (research methodology + experimental design + paper pipeline + research direction) with 3 metrics (papers + citations + benchmarks) and 5-criterion bar; the MLR's job is to run experiments rigorously, write papers, and contribute to research direction._

---

## 3. Why MLRs Fail Here

Five named failure modes of MLRs whose what an ml researcher actually does produced zero results.

- **The SWE-Only Failure.** MLR works as ML engineer. No research output.
- **The No-Experiments Failure.** 0 experiments per week. No rigor.
- **The No-Papers Failure.** 0 papers per year. No publication output.
- **The No-Code-Quality Failure.** Throwaway code. Not reproducible.
- **The No-Direction Failure.** No research direction. Reactive.

---

## 4. Mental Models

Four mental models that compress what an ml researcher actually does.

**mental model 1: The 4 MLR Pillars:** 4 pillars: research methodology + experimental design + paper pipeline + research direction.

**mental model 2: The 3 MLR Metrics:** 3 metrics: papers + citations + benchmarks.

**mental model 3: The 5-Criterion Research Bar:** 5 criteria: novel + rigorous + reproducible + impactful + honest.

**mental model 4: The MLR Weekly Cadence:** Mon-Fri experiments + write + review.

## 8. Failure Mode Postmortem

An MLR at a 200-person B2B AI company worked as ML engineer. 0 papers. 0 reproducible experiments. The senior scientist asked: 'Where is your publication output?' The MLR had no answer.

What the first MLR missed: what an ml researcher actually does is a system. The first MLR had no system. The second MLR had 4 mental models + 3 frameworks + 5 criteria. The system is the leverage.

The lesson: the MLR who has the system has what an ml researcher actually does. The MLR who has no system has the failure mode.
