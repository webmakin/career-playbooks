## 2. Problem

You are an MLR at acme-corp. The senior scientist has just told you: "research direction and vision. 30-day timeline."

This chapter tells you the 4 mental models, 3 frameworks, and 5-criterion quality bar.

**Decision in one sentence:** _MLR research direction contribution is a 4-input framework + 3-horizon model + 5-criterion bar; the MLR's job is to propose research direction, contribute to the research agenda, and own the execution of specific direction items._

---

## 3. Why MLRs Fail Here

Five named failure modes of MLRs whose research direction and vision produced zero results.

- **The No-Direction-Input Failure.** MLR has no direction input.
- **The 1-Horizon Failure.** NOW only. No NEXT + BEYOND.
- **The No-Hypothesis Failure.** No clear hypothesis. Ad-hoc experiments.
- **The No-Impact-Framing Failure.** No impact framing.
- **The No-Experiments-Aligned Failure.** Experiments don't match direction.

---

## 4. Mental Models

Four mental models that compress research direction and vision.

**mental model 1: The 4 Direction Inputs:** 4 inputs: research area + hypothesis + methodology + impact.

**mental model 2: The 3-Horizon Model:** 3 horizons: NOW (this quarter) + NEXT (next 4) + BEYOND (next 4 years).

**mental model 3: The 5-Criterion Direction Bar:** 5 criteria: novel + rigorous + impactful + actionable + owned.

**mental model 4: The Research Direction Memo:** 1-page memo template.

## 8. Failure Mode Postmortem

An MLR at a 200-person B2B AI company had no research direction input. Ran ad-hoc experiments. The senior scientist's direction was unclear. 0 papers.

What the first MLR missed: research direction and vision is a system. The first MLR had no system. The second MLR had 4 mental models + 3 frameworks + 5 criteria. The system is the leverage.

The lesson: the MLR who has the system has research direction and vision. The MLR who has no system has the failure mode.

## 9. Self-Assessment Rubric

| 1 | **4 direction inputs** | 0-1 | 2-3 | 4 inputs |
| 2 | **3 horizons** | 1 | 2 | 3 horizons |
| 3 | **5-criterion bar** | 0-2 | 3-4 | 5 criteria |
| 4 | **Hypothesis quality** | None | Partial | Falsifiable + impactful |
| 5 | **Direction alignment** | None | Partial | Every experiment tied to direction |


**Disqualifier:** any 1 on dimension 1 or 3. An MLR who has 0-1 inputs or 0-2 criteria is in the No-Direction-Input or No-Hypothesis failure mode.

**Total:** ___ / 25. **Pass threshold:** 18/25, no dimension below 3.
