# Chapter 4: The Engineering Org at Scale

## What this exercise is

A 90-minute practice exercise for the VP of Engineering role. You are the VPE at **acme-corp** and must produce a specific artifact (see below) using the 3 frameworks from Chapter 4.

## Files in this bundle

- `drill.md` — the scenario, your task, constraints, and grading rubric.
- `template.md` — a fill-in-the-blank template for your deliverable.
- `worked-example.md` — a fully completed drill, scored against the chapter's rubric.
- `README.md` — this file.

## How to use this bundle

1. **Read `drill.md` first.** Don't open the template or worked example until you've thought about the scenario.
2. **Use `template.md` to draft your deliverable.** Time-box to 90 minutes.
3. **Compare to `worked-example.md`.** Score yourself against the chapter's Self-Assessment Rubric.
4. **Iterate.** If you score below 18/25, re-draft using the chapter's mental models and frameworks.

## Deliverable

Save your completed drill as `portfolio/chapter-04-scale-transition-plan.md` in your personal portfolio. This is interview evidence — see Chapter 27 (Portfolio Map) for how this maps to specific interview questions.

## Estimated time

- Reading the chapter: 30-45 min
- Doing the drill: 90 min
- Self-assessment: 15 min
- Total: ~2-3 hours

## Prerequisites

- Read Chapter 4 in full.
- Skim Chapter 1 (decision stack, three horizons, health dashboard).
- Optional: skim the AI Engineering Director playbook's Ch 1 for the IC-to-Director category change, which is a useful reference for Ch 2's Director-to-VP shift.
