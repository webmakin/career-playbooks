# Worked Example: Security, Privacy, Compliance at Scale

> **Source:** VP of Engineering Playbook, Chapter 13
> **Scored: 22-23/25** (1 dimension at 3, all others 4+; passes the 18/25 threshold)

The full worked example for this chapter is in the chapter body under "## 7. Worked Example". The chapter's worked example IS the deliverable template for the drill. Use it as a reference for tone, structure, and content density.

Key patterns from the worked example:

1. **Concrete numbers throughout.** Every claim has a number ($, %, eq-qtr, time-to-recover, MTTR, etc.). Vague language fails the rubric.
2. **Trade-offs are explicit.** Every "yes" has a "no" or "defer" beside it. The "decline list" or "things we will NOT do" pattern recurs in every chapter.
3. **Owners are named.** Every SLO, service, vendor, or bet has a Director-level owner.
4. **The pushback is named.** The VPE names the 1 thing they'll push back on the CEO/CFO. Without it, dimension 5 is at 3.
5. **The first-meeting agenda is concrete.** Not "I'll set up security" but "60-min quarterly review, 10 standards, vendor review process."

To score 22+/25: hit all 5 patterns above.
