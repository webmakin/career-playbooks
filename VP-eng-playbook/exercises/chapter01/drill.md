## Scenario

You are the VP of Engineering at **acme-corp**, a 1,200-person B2B SaaS company. The CEO has just told you: "Engineering is the bottleneck on every roadmap commitment. Fix it." You have 5 Directors reporting in, 200 engineers in your portfolio, a $40M annual budget, and a board that has been hearing about engineering velocity for 24 months without seeing it. The first decision you make will set the tone for the next 24.

## Your task

Build a **24-month VPE plan** using the three frameworks from Chapter 1 (30/60/90 + Decision Stack + Health Dashboard):

1. **1-page diagnosis**: What's actually broken? Pick 3 problems.
2. **24-month strategy**: 3 horizons, 3 priorities per horizon.
3. **30/60/90 first 90 days**.
4. **5-metric dashboard** with targets.
5. **3 things you will NOT do** in the first 24 months.

## Constraints

- Under 1,200 words.
- Use the chapter's terminology (C-suite triangle, three horizons, org multiplier, decision stack, health dashboard).
- Every claim must be defensible with the chapter's evidence.
