# Worked Example: Engineering Auditability

> **Source:** VP of Engineering Playbook, Chapter 25
> **Scored: 22-23/25** (1 dimension at 3, all others 4+; passes the 18/25 threshold)

The full worked example for this chapter is in the chapter body under "## 7. Worked Example". The chapter's worked example IS the deliverable template for the drill. Use it as a reference for tone, structure, and content density.

Key patterns from the worked example:

1. **Concrete numbers throughout.** Every claim has a number ($, %, customers, ARR, gross margin, etc.). Vague language fails the rubric.
2. **Trade-offs are explicit.** Every "yes" has a "no" or "defer" beside it. The "decline list" pattern recurs.
3. **Owners are named.** Every decision, OKR, or pricing option has a VPE/CFO/CMO/Director-level owner.
4. **The pushback is named.** The VPE names the 1 thing they'll push back on. Without it, dimension 5 is at 3.
5. **The first-meeting agenda is concrete.** Not "I'll align with the CPO" but "Weekly 1:1 Tuesdays 10am, 30 min, agenda template."

To score 22+/25: hit all 5 patterns above.
