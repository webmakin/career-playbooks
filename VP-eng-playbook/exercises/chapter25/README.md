# Chapter 25: Engineering Auditability

## What this exercise is

A 90-minute practice exercise for the VP of Engineering role. You are the VPE at **acme-corp** and must produce a specific artifact (see below) using the 3 frameworks from Chapter 25.

## Files in this bundle

- `drill.md` — the scenario, your task, constraints, and grading rubric.
- `template.md` — a fill-in-the-blank template for your deliverable.
- `worked-example.md` — a reference to the chapter's worked example.
- `README.md` — this file.

## How to use this bundle

1. **Read `drill.md` first.** Don't open the template or chapter worked example until you've thought about the scenario.
2. **Use `template.md` to draft your deliverable.** Time-box to 90 minutes.
3. **Compare to the chapter's "## 7. Worked Example".** Score yourself against the chapter's Self-Assessment Rubric.
4. **Iterate.** If you score below 18/25, re-draft using the chapter's mental models and frameworks.

## Deliverable

Save your completed drill as `portfolio/chapter-25-audit-response.md` in your personal portfolio. This is interview evidence — see Chapter 27 (Portfolio Map) for how this maps to specific interview questions.

## Estimated time

- Reading the chapter: 30-45 min
- Doing the drill: 90 min
- Self-assessment: 15 min
- Total: ~2-3 hours

- Read Chapter 25 in full.
- Skim Chapter 1 (decision stack, three horizons, health dashboard).
- Skim earlier chapters if this chapter references them.
