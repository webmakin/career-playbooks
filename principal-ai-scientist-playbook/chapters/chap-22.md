## 2. Problem

You are a PAS at acme-corp. The CTO has just told you: "FY32 pas research risk management. 30-day timeline."

This chapter tells you the 4 mental models, 3 frameworks, and 5-criterion quality bar.

**Decision in one sentence:** _PAS research risk management is a 4-pillar system (paper + production + collaboration + compliance) with 3 risk tiers (high + medium + low) and 5-criterion bar; the PAS's job is to design the risk register, track the risks, and own the mitigation plans._

---

## 3. Why PASs Fail Here

Five named failure modes of PASs whose pas research risk management produced zero results.

- **The No-Risk-Register Failure.** No risk register.
- **The No-Paper-Owner Failure.** No paper owner. Missed submissions.
- **The No-Compliance Failure.** IRB / data compliance not tracked.
- **The No-Production-Risk Failure.** No production risk tracking.
- **The No-Mitigation Failure.** Risks identified, no mitigation.

---

## 4. Mental Models

Four mental models that compress pas research risk management.

**mental model 1: The 4 Risk Pillars:** 4 pillars: paper + production + collaboration + compliance.

**mental model 2: The 3 Risk Tiers:** 3 tiers: HIGH + MEDIUM + LOW.

**mental model 3: The 5-Criterion Bar:** 5 criteria: tracked + owned + timed + mitigated + reviewed.

**mental model 4: The Risk Register Template:** Per-risk detail.

## 8. Failure Mode Postmortem

A PAS at a 200-person B2B AI company had no risk register. NeurIPS submission missed deadline. Production model diverged 5%. Stanford advisor went on sabbatical.

What the first PAS missed: pas research risk management is a system. The first PAS had no system. The second PAS had 4 mental models + 3 frameworks + 5 criteria. The system is the leverage.

The lesson: the PAS who has the system has pas research risk management. The PAS who has no system has the failure mode.
