## 2. Problem

You are a PAS at acme-corp. The CTO has just told you: "FY32 pas research compliance and ethics. 30-day timeline."

**Decision in one sentence:** _PAS research compliance is a 4-pillar system (IRB + data + AI safety + open-source) with 3 audit cycles (annual + quarterly + continuous) and 5-criterion bar; the PAS's job is to design the compliance program, run the audits, and own the certification._

## 3. Why PASs Fail Here

Five named failure modes of PASs whose pas research compliance and ethics produced zero results.

- **The No-Compliance-Program Failure.** No compliance program.
- **The Annual-Only Failure.** Audits are annual.
- **The No-IRB Failure.** No IRB review for human-subjects research.
- **The No-AI-Safety Failure.** No AI safety review.
- **The No-Owner Failure.** No compliance owner.

## 8. Failure Mode Postmortem

A PAS at a 200-person B2B AI company had no compliance program. IRB review missed. AI safety eval failed. Customer trust eroded. $500K contract lost.

What the first PAS missed: pas research compliance and ethics is a system. The first PAS had no system. The second PAS had 4 mental models + 3 frameworks + 5 criteria. The system is the leverage.

The lesson: the PAS who has the system has pas research compliance and ethics. The PAS who has no system has the failure mode.
