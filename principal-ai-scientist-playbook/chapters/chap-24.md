## 3. Why PASs Fail Here

Five named failure modes of PASs whose pas research crisis response produced zero results.

- **The No-Crisis-Playbook Failure.** No playbook. Ad-hoc response.
- **The 6-Hour-Outage Failure.** MTTR was 6 hours.
- **The No-Customer-Comm Failure.** No customer comms.
- **The No-War-Room Failure.** No war room.
- **The No-Post-Crisis-Review Failure.** No post-crisis review.

## 4. Mental Models

Four mental models that compress pas research crisis response.

**mental model 3: The 5-Criterion Bar:** 5 criteria: detected + contained + resolved + communicated + reviewed.

## 8. Failure Mode Postmortem

A PAS at a 200-person B2B AI company had a 6-hour outage. No playbook. Ad-hoc response. Customer A churned. Academic collaborators lost trust.

What the first PAS missed: pas research crisis response is a system. The first PAS had no system. The second PAS had 4 mental models + 3 frameworks + 5 criteria. The system is the leverage.

The lesson: the PAS who has the system has pas research crisis response. The PAS who has no system has the failure mode.
