You are a PAS at acme-corp. The CTO has just told you: "FY33 research methodology appendix for pass. 30-day timeline."

**Decision in one sentence:** _PAS research methodology review is a 4-method research model (empirical + theoretical + benchmark + ablation) with 3 quality attributes (rigor + reproducibility + novelty) and 5-criterion methodology quality bar; the PAS's job is to review methodologies across all 4 methods, validate the 3 quality attributes, and own the 11-step methodology review process._

## 3. Why PASs Fail Here

Five named failure modes of PASs whose research methodology appendix for pass produced zero results.

- **The No-Methodology-Review Failure.** No methodology review process.
- **The 1-Method-Review Failure.** Only 1 method (e.g., empirical).
- **The No-Quality-Attribute Failure.** No rigor + reproducibility + novelty review.
- **The Ad-Hoc-Review Failure.** Reviews are ad-hoc.
- **The No-Methodology-Documentation Failure.** No methodology docs.

## 8. Failure Mode Postmortem

A PAS at a 200-person B2B AI company had no methodology review process. Empirical methods only. No ablation. Reproducibility broken. NeurIPS submissions rejected.

What the first PAS missed: research methodology appendix for pass is a system. The first PAS had no system. The second PAS had 4 mental models + 3 frameworks + 5 criteria. The system is the leverage.

The lesson: the PAS who has the system has research methodology appendix for pass. The PAS who has no system has the failure mode.
