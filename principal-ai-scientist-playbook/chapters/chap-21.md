## 2. Problem

You are a PAS at acme-corp. The CTO has just told you: "FY32 influencing at the research function. 30-day timeline."

This chapter tells you the 4 mental models, 3 frameworks, and 5-criterion quality bar.

**Decision in one sentence:** _PAS research influence is a 4-stakeholder system (CTO + VPE + PM + academic) with 3 influence patterns (data + framework + relationship) and 5-criterion influence bar; the PAS's job is to identify stakeholders, choose patterns, and own the research voice._

---

## 3. Why PASs Fail Here

Five named failure modes of PASs whose influencing at the research function produced zero results.

- **The No-Stakeholder-Map Failure.** No stakeholder map. Ad-hoc influence.
- **The Slack-Channel Failure.** Drops research input in Slack.
- **The No-Framework Failure.** Opinions, not frameworks.
- **The No-Data Failure.** Opinions, not data.
- **The No-Relationship Failure.** Only talks in crisis.

---

**mental model 3: The 5-Criterion Influence Bar:** 5 criteria: specific + aligned + timed + owned + reusable.

## 8. Failure Mode Postmortem

A PAS at a 200-person B2B AI company had no stakeholder map. Research input was dropped in Slack. The CTO was out of the loop. The PM was confused. 0 publications.

What the first PAS missed: influencing at the research function is a system. The first PAS had no system. The second PAS had 4 mental models + 3 frameworks + 5 criteria. The system is the leverage.

The lesson: the PAS who has the system has influencing at the research function. The PAS who has no system has the failure mode.
