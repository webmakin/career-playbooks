## 2. Problem

You are a PAS at acme-corp. The CTO has just told you: "FY31 pas research performance management at scale. 30-day timeline."

This chapter tells you the 4 mental models, 3 frameworks, and 5-criterion quality bar.

**Decision in one sentence:** _PAS research performance at scale is a 5-dim rubric + 3-tier calibration + quarterly cadence + 12-month dev plan per RS; the PAS's job is to design the rubric, calibrate, and own the development plans._

---

## 3. Why PASs Fail Here

Five named failure modes of PASs whose pas research performance management at scale produced zero results.

- **The SWE-Rubric Failure.** 1-2 dims only.
- **The No-Calibration Failure.** No cross-RS calibration.
- **The Annual-Only Failure.** Annual review. No course correction.
- **The No-Dev-Plan Failure.** No 12-month plan.
- **The No-Promo-Pipeline Failure.** No promotion criteria.

---

## 4. Mental Models

Four mental models that compress pas research performance management at scale.

**mental model 1: The 5-Dim Research Rubric:** 5 dims: publication + research depth + engineering + collaboration + impact.

**mental model 2: The 3-Tier Calibration:** 3 tiers across 10+ RSs.

**mental model 3: The Quarterly Cadence:** 4 quarters.

**mental model 4: The 12-Month Dev Plan:** 1 plan per RS.

## 8. Failure Mode Postmortem

A PAS at a 200-person B2B AI company had 10 RSs using publication-only rubric. Engineering + collaboration invisible. 3 best RSs left for 'Senior RS' titles elsewhere.

What the first PAS missed: pas research performance management at scale is a system. The first PAS had no system. The second PAS had 4 mental models + 3 frameworks + 5 criteria. The system is the leverage.

The lesson: the PAS who has the system has pas research performance management at scale. The PAS who has no system has the failure mode.
