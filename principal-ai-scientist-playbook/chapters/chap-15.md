## 2. Problem

You are a PAS at acme-corp. The CTO has just told you: "Contribute to FY27 research strategy. 5 architectures to evaluate. 3 papers to publish. 1 Stanford collaboration."

This chapter tells you the 4 mental models, 3 frameworks, and 5-criterion quality bar.

**Decision in one sentence:** _PAS research strategy contribution is a 4-input system (research + paper + architecture + collaboration) with 3 horizons (NOW + NEXT + BEYOND) and 5-criterion bar; the PAS's job is to provide high-quality research input, accept the CTO's strategy, and own the research roadmap._

---

## 3. Why PASs Fail Here

Five named failure modes of PASs whose pas research strategy at company scale produced zero results.

- **The No-Research-Input Failure.** PAS provides no research input. Strategy is product-only.
- **The 1-Horizon Failure.** PAS only plans NOW. No NEXT + BEYOND.
- **The No-Capacity-Input Failure.** PAS doesn't provide research capacity numbers.
- **The No-Paper-Targets Failure.** PAS doesn't address paper targets.
- **The No-Stakeholder-Influence Failure.** PAS only talks to CTO.

---

## 4. Mental Models

Four mental models that compress pas research strategy at company scale.

**mental model 1: The 4 Strategy Inputs:** 4 inputs the PAS provides: research agenda + paper targets + architecture decisions + collaborations.

**mental model 2: The 3 Horizon Levels:** 3 levels: NOW (this quarter), NEXT (next 4 quarters), BEYOND (next 4 years).

**mental model 3: The 5-Criterion Strategy Bar:** 5 criteria: specific + measured + owned + timed + novel.

**mental model 4: The 5-Stakeholder Strategy Map:** 5 stakeholders: CTO + VP Eng + PM + Stanford advisor + CEO.

What the first PAS missed: pas research strategy at company scale is a system. The first PAS had no system. The second PAS had 4 mental models + 3 frameworks + 5 criteria. The system is the leverage.
