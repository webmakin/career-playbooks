## 2. Problem

You are a PAS at acme-corp. The CTO has just told you: "FY31 pas research org design at scale. 30-day timeline."

This chapter tells you the 4 mental models, 3 frameworks, and 5-criterion quality bar.

**Decision in one sentence:** _PAS research org design is a 4-model system (functional + matrix + research-aligned + platform-aligned) with 3 team topologies (research + engineering + cross-functional) and 5-criterion org quality bar; the PAS's job is to design the research org, hire the team, and own the research capacity._

---

## 3. Why PASs Fail Here

Five named failure modes of PASs whose pas research org design at scale produced zero results.

- **The 1-Model Failure.** PAS uses 1 org model. Doesn't scale.
- **The Silo Failure.** 5 silos, no cross-team work. Research slips.
- **The No-EM Failure.** PAS has no EM. RSs unmanaged.
- **The No-Cross-Functional Failure.** Only functional teams. No research velocity.
- **The No-Platform Failure.** No platform team. RSs rebuild infra.

## 8. Failure Mode Postmortem

A PAS at a 200-person B2B AI company had 5 RSs in 1 silo (research only). 0 production models. The CTO asked: 'Where are the production models?' The PAS had no production team.

What the first PAS missed: pas research org design at scale is a system. The first PAS had no system. The second PAS had 4 mental models + 3 frameworks + 5 criteria. The system is the leverage.

The lesson: the PAS who has the system has pas research org design at scale. The PAS who has no system has the failure mode.
