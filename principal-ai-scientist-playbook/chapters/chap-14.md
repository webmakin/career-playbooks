## 2. Problem

You are a PAS at acme-corp. The CTO has just told you: "Design the research-PM partnership plan. PM Sarah has 5 product themes. PAS has 5 architectures to evaluate. 30-day timeline."

This chapter tells you the 4 mental models, 3 frameworks, and 5-criterion quality bar.

**Decision in one sentence:** _PAS research-PM partnership is a 4-boundary ownership model (PAS owns research agenda + paper targets + architecture decisions; PM owns product roadmap + customer narrative + launch timing) with 3 cadences and 5-stakeholder influence map; the PAS's job is to provide high-quality research input, accept PM's product decisions, and own the research-to-product handoff._

---

## 3. Why PASs Fail Here

Five named failure modes of PASs whose pas research-pm partnership produced zero results.

- **The No-Research-Input Failure.** The PAS drops research in Slack. PM gets overwhelmed.
- **The No-Paper-Target Failure.** No paper targets per quarter. PM can't plan launch.
- **The PM-Tells-Me-What-To-Research Failure.** PAS defers to PM on research priorities. Research is lost.
- **The PAS-Tells-PM-What-To-Build Failure.** PAS demands specific features. PM loses ownership.
- **The No-Cadence Failure.** PAS + PM only talk in crisis. Reactive partnership.

---

## 4. Mental Models

Four mental models that compress pas research-pm partnership.

**mental model 1: The 4 Ownership Boundaries:** 4 things, 2 owners. PAS owns research agenda + paper targets + architecture decisions + collaboration timing. PM owns product roadmap + customer narrative + launch timing.

**mental model 2: The 3 Cadence Patterns:** 3 ways to sync: weekly 30 min, monthly 60 min retro, quarterly 90 min roadmap review.

**mental model 3: The 1-Page Research Brief:** A 1-page research brief either lands or dies based on 7 criteria: hypothesis, methodology, expected results, paper target, production impact, effort, capacity.

**mental model 4: The 5-Stakeholder Influence Map:** 5 stakeholders: CTO (weekly), VP Eng (monthly), PM (weekly), Stanford advisor (quarterly), CEO (quarterly).

What the first PAS missed: pas research-pm partnership is a system. The first PAS had no system. The second PAS had 4 mental models + 3 frameworks + 5 criteria. The system is the leverage.
