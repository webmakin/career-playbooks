## 1. Epigraph

## 2. Problem

You are a PAS at acme-corp. The CTO has just told you: "5 papers/year target, 1 open-source library target, 2 benchmark submissions target. Research contributes $2M ARR."

This chapter tells you the 4 mental models, 3 frameworks, and 5-criterion quality bar.

**Decision in one sentence:** _PAS research revenue is a 4-pillar system (papers + open-source + benchmarks + industry voice) with 3 GTM motions (land + expand + retain) and 5-criterion revenue quality bar; the PAS's job is to align research with revenue, track the pillars, and own the industry voice._

---

## 3. Why PASs Fail Here

Five named failure modes of PASs whose pas research-as-revenue produced zero results.

- **The No-Revenue-Alignment Failure.** Research doesn't know revenue target.
- **The 1-GTM-Motion Failure.** Research supports only 1 motion.
- **The No-Customer-Outcome Failure.** No customer outcome tracking.
- **The No-Paper-Revenue-Linking Failure.** Papers not linked to revenue.
- **The No-Industry-Voice Failure.** No keynotes, panels, or talks.

---

## 4. Mental Models

Four mental models that compress pas research-as-revenue.

**mental model 1: The 4 Revenue Pillars:** 4 pillars: papers + open-source + benchmarks + industry voice.

**mental model 2: The 3 GTM Motions:** 3 motions: land (new logos via papers) + expand (upsell via OSS) + retain (churn reduction via benchmarks).

**mental model 3: The 5-Criterion Revenue Quality Bar:** 5 criteria: aligned + specific + measured + owned + timed.

**mental model 4: The Roadmap-to-Revenue Traceability:** 4-step chain: paper → citation → adoption → revenue.

## 8. Failure Mode Postmortem

A PAS at a 200-person B2B AI company had no research revenue alignment. 4 papers shipped but only 1 was tied to revenue ($200K ARR vs $2M target). The other 3 papers were features without revenue impact.

What the first PAS missed: pas research-as-revenue is a system. The first PAS had no system. The second PAS had 4 mental models + 3 frameworks + 5 criteria. The system is the leverage.

The lesson: the PAS who has [system] has pas research-as-revenue. The PAS who has [no system] has [bad outcome].
