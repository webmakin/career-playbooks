# Principal AI Scientist Playbook

## Preface: How to Read This Book

> **The Principal AI Scientist role is the operating layer between research and engineering at the AI frontier.** This book is for the senior ML engineer who wants to make the leap to PAS, the new PAS in their first 90 days, or the PAS scaling from 5 to 10 research scientists.

### How the book is organized

The book has **4 parts and 28 chapters**, plus this preface, glossary, and errata:

- **Part I — Foundations (Ch 1-9).** What a PAS is, the role, the IC-to-PAS category change, hiring, performance, career, comp, culture.
- **Part II — Execution (Ch 10-17).** Planning, sprint execution, quality, research-to-production, research-PM partnership, strategy, build vs buy, research-as-revenue.
- **Part III — Scale (Ch 18-21).** Org design at scale, hiring at scale, performance at scale, influence.
- **Part IV — Governance & Portfolio (Ch 22-28).** Risk, compliance, crisis, auditability, the 30/60/90, the portfolio map, methodology appendix.

### The 11-section chapter anatomy

Every chapter follows the same 11-section structure. This is the discipline.

1. **Epigraph** — the one-sentence thesis.
2. **Problem** — the situation + decision in one sentence.
3. **Why PASs Fail Here** — 5 named failure modes.
4. **Mental Models** — 4 mental models with diagrams.
5. **Frameworks** — 3 reusable frameworks.
6. **Drill** — a 90-minute hands-on exercise.
7. **Worked Example** — a real-world application.
8. **Failure Mode Postmortem** — what the failure looks like.
9. **Self-Assessment Rubric** — 5-dimension, 25-point rubric.
10. **Portfolio Artifact Note** — what to save as interview evidence.
11. **Interview Questions** — 5 questions you should be able to answer.

### How to read it

1. **If you're aspiring to be a PAS:** Read Part I + Part IV Chapter 27 (Portfolio Map).
2. **If you're a new PAS in your first 90 days:** Read Part I + Part IV Chapter 26 (30/60/90).
3. **If you're a PAS scaling 5 → 10 RSs:** Read Part III (Scale).

The book is not a one-time read. It's a 28-chapter operating system for the PAS role.

### A note on opinion

This book is opinionated. The 4-pillar research agenda (research + engineering + publication + collaboration) is one PAS's framework. Adapt what doesn't fit your context.

### Let's go.

The PAS role is one of the most leveraged roles in any AI-forward company. A PAS who runs 10 RSs at 3 papers/year with 90%+ retention and 5 architectures evaluated per year is a force multiplier. The 28 chapters in this book are the leverage. The system is the discipline. The outcome is the research output.
