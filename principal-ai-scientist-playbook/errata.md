# Principal AI Scientist Playbook

## Errata & Known Issues

> **Honest accounting of what's incomplete, what might be wrong, and what the author would change in v2.**

### What's incomplete

- **Chapter 28 (Research Methodology Appendix) is an appendix.** It introduces the 4-method model + 3 quality attributes but doesn't go deep on each method. The PAS should pair this with research-methodology-specific reading.

- **No case studies of named PAS transitions.** The chapters describe what good PASs do, but don't include specific named case studies. v2 would add 5-10 named case studies.

- **No coverage of remote research orgs.** All chapters assume in-person. v2 would add a chapter on remote + distributed research.

### What might be wrong

- **The 4-pillar research agenda is one PAS's framework.** Some PASs prefer 3-pillar (research + publication + collaboration) or 5-pillar (with industry voice as a separate pillar). The book uses 4-pillar because it's the most common.

- **The 5 architectures per year target.** Some PASs ship 2-3 per year, others ship 10+. The book uses 5 because it's the median for a PAS at a 200-person AI company.

- **Some Mermaid diagrams have visual artifacts.** mdBook's Mermaid renderer occasionally has issues with complex diagrams.

### What the author would change in v2

- **Add a chapter on AI safety + governance.** The book touches on AI safety in Ch 23 but doesn't go deep. v2 would have a dedicated chapter on AI safety reviews, model cards, and responsible AI.

- **Add a chapter on cross-functional collaboration.** The book covers research-PM partnership (Ch 14) but not research-engineering or research-sales.

- **Add case studies.** 5-10 named case studies with permission.

- **Tighten the 5-criterion bar.** The book uses different 5-criterion bars in different chapters. v2 would standardize.
