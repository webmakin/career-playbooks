### Research Agenda

- **PAS (Principal AI Scientist):** Senior scientist hybrid who owns the research agenda, architectural decisions, and academic relationships.
- **4-pillar research agenda:** Research + engineering + publication + collaboration.
- **Research roadmap:** 5-year vision + annual roadmap + quarterly OKRs.
- **5 architectures per year:** The PAS evaluates 5 model architectures per year (Transformer, Mamba, RWKV, MoE, Hyena).
- **3 papers per year:** Target publication output (1 per quarter at NeurIPS/ICML/ACL).
- **Architecture decision matrix:** 4-factor decision (cost + differentiation + scalability + novelty).

### Architecture & Modeling

- **Transformer:** Baseline attention-based architecture.
- **Mamba:** State-space model (linear scaling, 5x cost reduction).
- **RWKV:** Linear attention model.
- **MoE:** Mixture of experts (sparse, 10x scale).
- **Hyena:** Long convolution architecture (long context).

### Publication

- **NeurIPS / ICML / ACL:** Top-tier ML/NLP venues.
- **5-criterion paper bar:** Specific + measured + owned + timed + novel.
- **Paper ADR:** Architecture Decision Record for paper submissions.
- **Open-source release:** Library release tied to paper (industry voice + citations).

### Career & Comp

- **5-level RS ladder:** RS1 (Junior) → RS2 (Mid) → RS3 (Senior) → RS4 (Staff) → RS5 (Principal).
- **5-criterion promotion packet:** Papers + novelty + engineering + collaboration + impact.
- **4-pillar comp:** Base + bonus + equity + signing bonus.
- **Compete-ratio:** Total comp / market median. Target: 0.95-1.05.
- **Signing bonus:** One-time payment for RS3+ hires ($50K-$200K).

### Pipeline & Production

- **3-stage model lifecycle:** Research → staging → production.
- **Production parity:** Research accuracy == production accuracy (within 1%).
- **4-pillar pipeline:** Model dev + evaluation + serving + monitoring.
- **A/B test plan:** Production rollout via 1% → 10% → 100% traffic.

### Culture & Team

- **4 culture pillars:** Curiosity + rigor + collaboration + publication.
- **3 health metrics:** Papers + impact + retention.
- **5-criterion culture bar:** Per RS.
- **Experiment log template:** Per-experiment reproducibility record.

### Planning & Execution

- **4 horizons:** Annual + quarterly + monthly + weekly.
- **3 roadmap levels:** 5-year + annual + quarterly.
- **5-criterion research bar:** Specific + measured + owned + timed + novel.
- **4 sprint ceremonies:** Experiment planning + daily research sync + weekly demo + monthly retro.

### Stakeholders & Influence

- **4 stakeholder groups:** CTO + VPE + PM + academic.
- **3 influence patterns:** Data + framework + relationship.
- **5-criterion influence bar:** Specific + aligned + timed + owned + reusable.

### Governance & Risk

- **4 risk pillars:** Paper + production + collaboration + compliance.
- **3 risk tiers:** HIGH + MEDIUM + LOW.
- **4 compliance pillars:** IRB + data + AI safety + open-source.
- **4-phase crisis playbook:** Detect + contain + resolve + communicate.
