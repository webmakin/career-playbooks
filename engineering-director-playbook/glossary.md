### Org & People

- **EM (Engineering Manager):** The manager of 3-7 ICs. Reports to ED. Owns 1:1s, performance reviews, hiring for the team.
- **IC (Individual Contributor):** An engineer without direct reports. IC1 (Junior) through IC5 (Principal).
- **Stream-aligned team:** A cross-functional team aligned to a value stream, with end-to-end ownership of a product area.
- **Enabling team:** A specialist team (security, data, quality) that helps stream-aligned teams.
- **Complicated-subsystem team:** A specialist team that owns a complex subsystem (ML serving, infrastructure).
- **60-20-20 split:** The recommended org topology: 60% stream-aligned, 20% enabling, 20% complicated-subsystem.
- **EM-to-IC ratio:** The number of ICs per EM. Target: 1:5-1:7.
- **EM ladder:** 4-level EM ladder (EM1 Manager, EM2 Senior Manager, EM3 Director, EM4 Senior Director).

### Performance & Comp

- **5-dim rubric:** The 5-dimension performance rubric: technical depth, ownership, collaboration, leadership, growth.
- **3-tier calibration:** The 3-tier performance calibration: exceeds, meets, below.
- **12-month dev plan:** A 12-month development plan per engineer with top 3 areas + quarterly actions.
- **5-criterion promotion packet:** Promotion packet with 5 elements: volume, quality, impact, leadership, strategic influence.
- **4-pillar comp:** 4-pillar compensation: base, bonus, equity, benefits.
- **3 promo cycles:** 3 promotion cycles per year: Q4 annual, Q2 mid-year, spot.
- **Compa-ratio:** Compensation ratio (total comp / market median). Target: 0.95-1.05.

### Planning & Execution

- **4 planning horizons:** Annual + quarterly + monthly + weekly planning.
- **3 roadmap levels:** Company + function + team roadmap.
- **5-criterion roadmap bar:** 5-criterion roadmap quality bar: aligned, specific, measured, owned, timed.
- **4 sprint ceremonies:** Sprint planning, daily standup, sprint review, retrospective.
- **3 delivery metrics:** Velocity, quality, predictability.
- **5-criterion sprint bar:** 5-criterion sprint quality bar.
- **4-horizon cadence:** Annual + quarterly + monthly + weekly cadence.
- **Q[N] OKRs:** Quarterly Objectives and Key Results.

### Quality & Reliability

- **4 quality pillars:** Testing, code review, observability, incident response.
- **3 reliability metrics:** Uptime, MTTR, error rate.
- **5-criterion quality bar:** Test coverage, code review, PR cycle time, bug escape rate, MTTR.
- **80%+ coverage:** Test coverage target of 80%+.
- **99.9% uptime:** Reliability target of 99.9% (or "three nines").
- **MTTR (Mean Time To Recover):** The average time to recover from an incident.

### Incident Response

- **4-phase playbook:** Detect + contain + resolve + learn.
- **3-tier on-call:** L1 (junior) + L2 (senior) + L3 (principal) on-call tiers.
- **5-fact postmortem:** 5-fact postmortem: what, when, customer impact, root cause, action items.
- **MTTD (Mean Time To Detect):** The average time to detect an incident.
- **SEV1/2/3:** Severity levels. SEV1 = major, SEV2 = moderate, SEV3 = minor.

### ED-PM Partnership

- **4 ownership boundaries:** ED owns engineering capacity + delivery + technical roadmap. PM owns prioritization + product roadmap + customer narrative.
- **3 cadences:** Weekly sync + biweekly retro + quarterly roadmap review.
- **1-page PRFA:** 1-page engineering PRFA (problem, impact, workaround, solution, alternatives, recommendation, effort, capacity).
- **Land-or-die test:** A 1-page PRFA either lands or dies based on 7 criteria.
- **5-stakeholder influence map:** 5 stakeholders: PM + CPO + CSO + CEO + VP Eng.
- **Weekly sync agenda:** 5-section agenda: highlights, capacity, delivery, questions, decisions.

### Strategy & Revenue

- **4 strategy inputs:** Capacity + delivery + tech debt + innovation.
- **3 horizons:** NOW + NEXT + BEYOND.
- **5-criterion strategy bar:** Specific + measured + owned + timed + aligned.
- **5-stakeholder strategy map:** 5 stakeholders: VP Eng + CEO + CPO + CSO + CFO.
- **$5M ARR target:** Engineering revenue target tied to product launches, reliability, integrations, AI features.

### Build vs Buy

- **4-factor decision framework:** Cost + differentiation + timeline + maintenance.
- **3 buy scenarios:** Commodity (always buy) + clear leader (usually buy) + parity (case-by-case).
- **5-criterion build bar:** Differentiation + cost + timeline + maintenance + talent.
- **TCO (Total Cost of Ownership):** 3-year total cost: build IC cost + EM ongoing vs buy license + integration.

### Compliance & Risk

- **4 compliance pillars:** SOC 2 + GDPR + HIPAA + access control.
- **3 audit cycles:** Annual + quarterly + continuous.
- **5-criterion compliance bar:** Documented + owned + tested + reviewed + certified.
- **SOC 2 Type II:** Service Organization Control 2 Type II audit. 12-month audit window.
- **RBAC (Role-Based Access Control):** Access control based on user roles.
- **28-risk register:** Risk register with 28 risks across 4 pillars.

### Influence & Communication

- **4 stakeholder groups:** Peer EDs/VPs + PM/CPO + VP Eng/exec + direct reports.
- **3 influence patterns:** Data + framework + relationship.
- **5-criterion influence bar:** Specific + aligned + timed + owned + reusable.
- **Customer comms every 30 min:** Customer communication cadence during crisis.

### Portfolio & Career

- **4-part portfolio:** 4-part portfolio: Foundations + Execution + Scale + Governance.
- **11 chapter categories:** 11 chapter categories covering the ED role.
- **5-criterion portfolio bar:** Specific + measured + owned + timed + outcome.
- **30/60/90 plan:** 30-day listen + assess, 60-day design + align, 90-day execute + review.
- **Top 3 issues memo:** A memo identifying the top 3 issues in the first 30 days.
