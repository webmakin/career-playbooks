## 2. Problem

You are a Staff Engineer at acme-corp. The engineering director has just told you: "staff engineer performance management. 30-day timeline."

This chapter tells you the 4 mental models, 3 frameworks, and 5-criterion quality bar.

**Decision in one sentence:** _SE performance participation is a 5-dim rubric + 3-tier calibration + quarterly cadence + 12-month dev plan; the SE's job is to track own performance, contribute to calibration, and own the dev plan._

---

## 3. Why Staff Engineers Fail Here

Five named failure modes of Staff Engineers whose staff engineer performance management produced zero results.

- **The SWE-Rubric Failure.** 1-2 dims only.
- **The No-Self-Tracking Failure.** No self-tracking.
- **The No-Cadence Failure.** Annual only.
- **The No-Dev-Plan Failure.** No 12-month plan.
- **The No-Promo-Pipeline Failure.** No promotion criteria.

---

## 4. Mental Models

Four mental models that compress staff engineer performance management.

**mental model 1: The 5-Dim SE Rubric:** 5 dims: architecture + tech direction + quality + influence + impact.

**mental model 2: The 3-Tier Calibration:** 3 tiers.

**mental model 3: The Quarterly Cadence:** 4 quarters.

**mental model 4: The 12-Month Dev Plan:** 1 plan per SE.

## 8. Failure Mode Postmortem

An SE at a 200-person company had no self-tracking. The annual review was a surprise. The SE was below bar.

What the first Staff Engineer missed: staff engineer performance management is a system. The first SE had no system. The second SE had 4 mental models + 3 frameworks + 5 criteria. The system is the leverage.

The lesson: the SE who has the system has staff engineer performance management. The SE who has no system has the failure mode.
