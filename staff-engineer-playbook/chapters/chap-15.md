## 2. Problem

You are a Staff Engineer at acme-corp. The engineering director has just told you: "engineering strategy at company scale. 30-day timeline."

This chapter tells you the 4 mental models, 3 frameworks, and 5-criterion quality bar.

**Decision in one sentence:** _SE engineering strategy contribution is a 4-input framework + 3 horizon levels + 5-criterion bar; the SE's job is to provide high-quality technical input, accept the VP Eng's strategy, and own specific strategy items._

---

## 3. Why Staff Engineers Fail Here

Five named failure modes of Staff Engineers whose engineering strategy at company scale produced zero results.

- **The No-Strategy-Input Failure.** SE has no strategy input.
- **The 1-Horizon Failure.** NOW only.
- **The No-Architecture-Vision Failure.** No architecture vision.
- **The No-Stakeholder-Influence Failure.** No influence map.
- **The Reactive-Work Failure.** Reactive, not proactive.

---

## 4. Mental Models

Four mental models that compress engineering strategy at company scale.

**mental model 1: The 4 Strategy Inputs:** 4 inputs: architecture + tech direction + tooling + standards.

**mental model 2: The 3 Horizon Levels:** 3 levels: NOW + NEXT + BEYOND.

**mental model 3: The 5-Criterion Bar:** 5 criteria: specific + measured + owned + timed + aligned.

**mental model 4: The Strategy Memo:** 1-page memo template.

## 8. Failure Mode Postmortem

An SE at a 200-person company had no strategy input. The VP Eng's direction was unclear. 0 architecture decisions.

What the first Staff Engineer missed: engineering strategy at company scale is a system. The first SE had no system. The second SE had 4 mental models + 3 frameworks + 5 criteria. The system is the leverage.

The lesson: the SE who has the system has engineering strategy at company scale. The SE who has no system has the failure mode.
