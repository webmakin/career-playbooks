## 2. Problem

You are a Staff Engineer at acme-corp. The engineering director has just told you: "engineering revenue impact. 30-day timeline."

This chapter tells you the 4 mental models, 3 frameworks, and 5-criterion quality bar.

**Decision in one sentence:** _SE engineering revenue alignment is a 4-pillar system + 3 GTM motions + 5-criterion bar; the SE's job is to align engineering with revenue, track the pillars, and own the engineering value._

---

## 3. Why Staff Engineers Fail Here

Five named failure modes of Staff Engineers whose engineering revenue impact produced zero results.

- **The No-Revenue-Alignment Failure.** Engineering doesn't know revenue.
- **The 1-GTM-Motion Failure.** Engineering supports only 1 motion.
- **The No-Customer-Outcome Failure.** No customer outcome tracking.
- **The No-Adoption-Tracking Failure.** No adoption tracking.
- **The No-Expansion Failure.** No expansion tracking.

---

## 4. Mental Models

Four mental models that compress engineering revenue impact.

**mental model 1: The 4 Revenue Pillars:** 4 pillars: features + reliability + cost + adoption.

**mental model 2: The 3 GTM Motions:** 3 motions: land + expand + retain.

**mental model 3: The 5-Criterion Bar:** 5 criteria: aligned + specific + measured + owned + timed.

**mental model 4: The Revenue Traceability Chain:** Feature -> reliability -> revenue.

## 8. Failure Mode Postmortem

An SE at a 200-person company had no revenue alignment. 5 features shipped but only 1 was tied to revenue ($200K ARR vs $1M target).

What the first Staff Engineer missed: engineering revenue impact is a system. The first SE had no system. The second SE had 4 mental models + 3 frameworks + 5 criteria. The system is the leverage.

The lesson: the SE who has the system has engineering revenue impact. The SE who has no system has the failure mode.
