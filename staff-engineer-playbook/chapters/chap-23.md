## 2. Problem

## 3. Why Staff Engineers Fail Here

Five named failure modes of Staff Engineers whose engineering compliance and ethics produced zero results.

- **The No-Compliance-Awareness Failure.** No compliance awareness.
- **The Annual-Only Failure.** Annual review only.
- **The No-Security-Review Failure.** No security review.
- **The No-Privacy-Review Failure.** No privacy review.
- **The No-Owner Failure.** No compliance owner per SE.

## 4. Mental Models

Four mental models that compress engineering compliance and ethics.

**mental model 1: The 4 Compliance Pillars:** 4 pillars: security + privacy + data governance + code provenance.

**mental model 2: The 3 Audit Cycles:** 3 cycles: annual + quarterly + continuous.

**mental model 3: The 5-Criterion Bar:** 5 criteria: documented + owned + tested + reviewed + certified.

**mental model 4: The Compliance Roadmap:** 12-month timeline.

## 8. Failure Mode Postmortem

An SE at a 200-person company had no compliance awareness. Security review missed. Customer data leaked. The VP Eng said: 'Security is non-negotiable.'

What the first Staff Engineer missed: engineering compliance and ethics is a system. The first SE had no system. The second SE had 4 mental models + 3 frameworks + 5 criteria. The system is the leverage.

The lesson: the SE who has the system has engineering compliance and ethics. The SE who has no system has the failure mode.
