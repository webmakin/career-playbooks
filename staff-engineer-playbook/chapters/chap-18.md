## 2. Problem

You are a Staff Engineer at acme-corp. The engineering director has just told you: "staff engineer org design at scale. 30-day timeline."

This chapter tells you the 4 mental models, 3 frameworks, and 5-criterion quality bar.

**Decision in one sentence:** _SE org contribution is a 4-model system + 3 team topologies + 5-criterion bar; the SE's job is to provide input on org design, contribute to cross-team work, and own the engineering capacity._

---

## 3. Why Staff Engineers Fail Here

Five named failure modes of Staff Engineers whose staff engineer org design at scale produced zero results.

- **The 1-Model Failure.** 1 org model.
- **The Silo Failure.** Silos. No cross-team.
- **The No-EM Failure.** No EM.
- **The No-Cross-Functional Failure.** Only functional teams.
- **The No-Platform Failure.** No platform team.

## 8. Failure Mode Postmortem

An SE at a 200-person company was in a silo. 0 cross-team work. 0 architecture decisions.

What the first Staff Engineer missed: staff engineer org design at scale is a system. The first SE had no system. The second SE had 4 mental models + 3 frameworks + 5 criteria. The system is the leverage.

The lesson: the SE who has the system has staff engineer org design at scale. The SE who has no system has the failure mode.
