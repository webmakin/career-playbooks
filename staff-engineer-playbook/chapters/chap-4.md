## 2. Problem

You are a Staff Engineer at acme-corp. The engineering director has just told you: "staff engineer stakeholder management. 30-day timeline."

This chapter tells you the 4 mental models, 3 frameworks, and 5-criterion quality bar.

**Decision in one sentence:** _SE stakeholder management is a 4-group stakeholder map + 3 cadence patterns + 5-criterion bar; the SE's job is to identify stakeholders, run the cadences, and own the relationships._

---

## 3. Why Staff Engineers Fail Here

Five named failure modes of Staff Engineers whose staff engineer stakeholder management produced zero results.

- **The No-Stakeholder-Map Failure.** No stakeholder map.
- **The Slack-Channel Failure.** Drops architecture in Slack.
- **The No-EM-Relationship Failure.** Doesn't engage with EMs.
- **The No-Senior-IC-Mentorship Failure.** Doesn't mentor senior ICs.
- **The No-Customer-Outcome Failure.** Doesn't tie architecture to customers.

---

## 4. Mental Models

Four mental models that compress staff engineer stakeholder management.

**mental model 1: The 4 Stakeholder Groups:** 4 groups: VP Eng + EM + senior IC + customers.

**mental model 2: The 3 Cadence Patterns:** 3 cadences: weekly 1:1 with VP + monthly with EM + quarterly with senior IC.

**mental model 3: The 5-Criterion Bar:** 5 criteria: aligned + specific + measured + owned + reusable.

**mental model 4: The Stakeholder Map:** Per-stakeholder detail.

## 8. Failure Mode Postmortem

An SE at a 200-person company had no stakeholder map. Dropped architecture in Slack. VP Eng unaware. Architecture drifted.

What the first Staff Engineer missed: staff engineer stakeholder management is a system. The first SE had no system. The second SE had 4 mental models + 3 frameworks + 5 criteria. The system is the leverage.

The lesson: the SE who has the system has staff engineer stakeholder management. The SE who has no system has the failure mode.
