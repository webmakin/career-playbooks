## 2. Problem

You are a Staff Engineer at acme-corp. The engineering director has just told you: "hiring and onboarding. 30-day timeline."

This chapter tells you the 4 mental models, 3 frameworks, and 5-criterion quality bar.

**Decision in one sentence:** _SE hiring contribution is a 4-trait system + 3-stage loop + 5-step onboarding + 30-day ramp; the SE's job is to participate in interviews, mentor new hires, and own the onboarding experience._

---

## 3. Why Staff Engineers Fail Here

Five named failure modes of Staff Engineers whose hiring and onboarding produced zero results.

- **The No-Interview-Participation Failure.** SE doesn't interview.
- **The No-Mentorship Failure.** No mentorship.
- **The No-Onboarding Failure.** No structured onboarding.
- **The 30-Day-Ramp Failure.** No ramp-up.
- **The No-Hire-Bar-Input Failure.** No input on hire bar.

---

## 4. Mental Models

Four mental models that compress hiring and onboarding.

**mental model 1: The 4 Hire Traits:** 4 traits: architecture + tech direction + quality + collaboration.

**mental model 2: The 3-Stage Loop:** 3 stages: coding challenge + system design + architecture talk.

**mental model 3: The 5-Step Onboarding:** 5 steps: orient + first architecture + mentor + lead architecture + independent.

**mental model 4: The 30-Day Ramp:** Weekly goals.

## 8. Failure Mode Postmortem

An SE at a 200-person company didn't participate in interviews. New hires misaligned. 30% attrition in 90 days.

What the first Staff Engineer missed: hiring and onboarding is a system. The first SE had no system. The second SE had 4 mental models + 3 frameworks + 5 criteria. The system is the leverage.

The lesson: the SE who has the system has hiring and onboarding. The SE who has no system has the failure mode.
