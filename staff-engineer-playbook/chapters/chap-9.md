## 2. Problem

You are a Staff Engineer at acme-corp. The engineering director has just told you: "technical mentorship and apprenticeship. 30-day timeline."

This chapter tells you the 4 mental models, 3 frameworks, and 5-criterion quality bar.

**Decision in one sentence:** _SE technical mentorship is a 4-pillar framework + 3 mentorship templates + 5-criterion bar; the SE's job is to design the mentorship program, run the cadences, and own the engineering growth._

---

## 3. Why Staff Engineers Fail Here

Five named failure modes of Staff Engineers whose technical mentorship and apprenticeship produced zero results.

- **The No-Mentorship Failure.** No mentorship.
- **The 1:1-Only Failure.** Only 1:1. No code review.
- **The No-Pairing Failure.** No pairing sessions.
- **The No-Career-Planning Failure.** No career planning.
- **The No-Mentee-Outcomes Failure.** No mentee outcomes.

## 8. Failure Mode Postmortem

An SE at a 200-person company didn't mentor. Senior ICs left for Staff titles elsewhere. Retention dropped from 90% to 80%.

What the first Staff Engineer missed: technical mentorship and apprenticeship is a system. The first SE had no system. The second SE had 4 mental models + 3 frameworks + 5 criteria. The system is the leverage.

The lesson: the SE who has the system has technical mentorship and apprenticeship. The SE who has no system has the failure mode.
