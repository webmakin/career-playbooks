# AI Engineer Playbook

## Preface: How to Read This Book

> **The AI Engineer role is the operating layer between LLM APIs and customer-facing features at the AI product frontier.** This book is for the backend engineer who wants to make the leap to AIE, the new AIE in their first 90 days, or the AIE shipping AI features at scale.

### How the book is organized

The book has **4 parts and 28 chapters**, plus this preface, glossary, and errata:

- **Part I — Foundations (Ch 1-9).** What an AIE is, the role, the IC-to-AIE category change, LLM stack selection, stakeholder management, prompt engineering, RAG, agents, observability, cost.
- **Part II — Execution (Ch 10-17).** Serving, eval harness, safety, fine-tuning, AIE-PM partnership, strategy, build vs buy, revenue alignment.
- **Part III — Scale (Ch 18-21).** Org contribution, hiring, performance, influence.
- **Part IV — Governance & Portfolio (Ch 22-28).** Risk, compliance, crisis, auditability, the 30/60/90, the portfolio map, methodology appendix.

### The 11-section chapter anatomy

Every chapter follows the same 11-section structure:

1. **Epigraph** — the one-sentence thesis.
2. **Problem** — the situation + decision in one sentence.
3. **Why AIEs Fail Here** — 5 named failure modes.
4. **Mental Models** — 4 mental models with diagrams.
5. **Frameworks** — 3 reusable frameworks.
6. **Drill** — a 90-minute hands-on exercise.
7. **Worked Example** — a real-world application.
8. **Failure Mode Postmortem** — what the failure looks like.
9. **Self-Assessment Rubric** — 5-dimension, 25-point rubric.
10. **Portfolio Artifact Note** — what to save as interview evidence.
11. **Interview Questions** — 5 questions you should be able to answer.

### How to read it

1. **If you're aspiring to be an AIE:** Read Part I + Part IV Chapter 27 (Portfolio Map).
2. **If you're a new AIE in your first 90 days:** Read Part I + Part IV Chapter 26 (30/60/90).
3. **If you're an AIE scaling 5 → 15 features:** Read Part II (Execution).

### A note on opinion

This book is opinionated. The 4-pillar AIE model (LLM integration + prompt engineering + AI feature delivery + observability) is one AIE's framework. Adapt what doesn't fit your context.

### Let's go.

The AIE role is one of the most leveraged roles in any AI-forward company. An AIE who ships 5 AI features per quarter at <200ms p99 with 100% eval coverage and <1% jailbreak success rate is a force multiplier. The 28 chapters in this book are the leverage. The system is the discipline. The outcome is the AI feature output.
