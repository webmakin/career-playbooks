# Staff Engineer Playbook

## Preface: How to Read This Book

> **The Staff Engineer role is the principal IC engineering role - the technical leader who sets direction, drives cross-team architecture, and owns engineering excellence at company scale.** This book is for the senior engineer who wants to make the leap to SE, the new SE in their first 90 days, or the SE scaling from 1 team to cross-team influence.

### How the book is organized

The book has **4 parts and 28 chapters**, plus this preface, glossary, and errata:

- **Part I — Foundations (Ch 1-9).** What an SE is, the role, the IC-to-SE category change, technical direction, stakeholder management, architecture design, code review, tooling, reliability, mentorship.
- **Part II — Execution (Ch 10-17).** System design, tech debt, migrations, ops, SE-PM partnership, strategy, build vs buy, revenue alignment.
- **Part III — Scale (Ch 18-21).** Org contribution, hiring, performance, influence.
- **Part IV — Governance & Portfolio (Ch 22-28).** Risk, compliance, crisis, auditability, the 30/60/90, the portfolio map, methodology appendix.

### The 11-section chapter anatomy

Every chapter follows the same 11-section structure:

1. **Epigraph** — the one-sentence thesis.
2. **Problem** — the situation + decision in one sentence.
3. **Why Staff Engineers Fail Here** — 5 named failure modes.
4. **Mental Models** — 4 mental models with diagrams.
5. **Frameworks** — 3 reusable frameworks.
6. **Drill** — a 90-minute hands-on exercise.
7. **Worked Example** — a real-world application.
8. **Failure Mode Postmortem** — what the failure looks like.
9. **Self-Assessment Rubric** — 5-dimension, 25-point rubric.
10. **Portfolio Artifact Note** — what to save as interview evidence.
11. **Interview Questions** — 5 questions you should be able to answer.

### How to read it

1. **If you're aspiring to be an SE:** Read Part I + Part IV Chapter 27 (Portfolio Map).
2. **If you're a new SE in your first 90 days:** Read Part I + Part IV Chapter 26 (30/60/90).
3. **If you're an SE scaling 1 → 3 cross-team projects:** Read Part II (Execution).

### A note on opinion

This book is opinionated. The 4-pillar SE model (technical direction + cross-team architecture + engineering influence + technical mentorship) is one SE's framework. Adapt what doesn't fit your context.

### Let's go.

The SE role is one of the most leveraged roles in any engineering-forward company. An SE who sets technical direction across 3+ teams, drives 3+ architecture decisions per quarter, mentors 3+ senior ICs, and ships engineering excellence at 99.9% reliability is a force multiplier. The 28 chapters in this book are the leverage. The system is the discipline. The outcome is the engineering excellence.
