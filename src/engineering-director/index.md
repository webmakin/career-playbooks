# Engineering Director Playbook

> **Coming soon** — A practice-first guide for Engineering Directors outside the AI specialty. Same 11-section chapter anatomy, same exercise bundle discipline as the AI Eng Director and VP Eng playbooks.

This playbook follows the same 7 Parts / 28 chapters structure:

- **Part I — Foundations** (Ch 1-4): What an ED does, IC-to-ED change, org design, stakeholder management
- **Part II — Engineering Leadership** (Ch 5-9): Hiring, performance, ladders, comp, team health
- **Part III — Engineering Execution** (Ch 10-13): Planning, sprint execution, quality, incidents
- **Part IV — Engineering & Product** (Ch 14-17): Product partnership, strategy, build vs buy, GTM
- **Part V — Engineering at Scale** (Ch 18-21): Org design at scale, talent growth, performance, exec influence
- **Part VI — Governance & Risk** (Ch 22-25): Risk, regulatory, crisis, auditability
- **Part VII — The ED's Portfolio** (Ch 26-28): 30/60/90, portfolio map, system design appendix

All 28 stub chapters live at https://webmakin.github.io/career-playbooks/engineering-director/

The full chapters are shipping one phase at a time. See [STATUS.md](https://github.com/webmakin/career-playbooks/blob/main/STATUS.md) for current progress.
