# Which Playbook Is For You?

A 30-second decision tree:

- **Are you targeting Director or Head of AI?** → [AI Engineering Director](../ai-eng-director/README.md)
- **Are you targeting Director in any function?** → Engineering Director (coming soon)
- **VP / SVP?** → VP of Engineering (coming soon)
- **Principal / Distinguished Engineer (AI research-adjacent)?** → Principal AI Scientist (coming soon)
- **Research-track ML?** → ML Researcher (coming soon)
- **IC, shipping AI features?** → AI Engineer (coming soon)
- **Staff Engineer, general track?** → Staff Engineer (coming soon)

If you're not sure which one to pick, ask yourself:

1. **What decision am I making this month that the playbook needs to sharpen?** (Concrete scenario beats abstract title.)
2. **Whose job am I reverse-engineering?** (Director ≠ Engineer ≠ Researcher. The role you're targeting is the playbook to read.)
3. **What artifact do I need for an interview or 30/60/90 plan?** (Each playbook ends with a portfolio map showing which artifact answers which question.)

If you still can't decide, start with the [AI Engineering Director](../ai-eng-director/README.md) — the meta-frameworks (Decision Memo, Reversibility Ladder, Moat Test) apply across all senior roles, even if the technical chapters don't.
