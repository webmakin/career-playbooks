## 3. Why FDEs Fail Here

- **The 10x IC Mentality.** The FDE evaluates their own impact by personal technical output (lines of code, features shipped), not by the customer's outcome.

- **The PM Mentality.** The FDE acts like a PM, owning the product roadmap and not shipping code. The customer doesn't get a working deployment.

- **The Sales-Engineer Mentality.** The FDE acts like a sales engineer, demoing the product and answering questions without owning the deployment.
